Welcome to your burrow

A quiet place for the things you want to read

This is your burrow. Anything you save here is kept on your phone as clean text, so it opens fast, reads the same every time, and works with no signal at all. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose it to.

Save an article

There are two ways. In your browser, or in any app that shares links, tap Share and choose Burrow. The link is saved at once and the article downloads in the background, so you can go straight back to what you were doing. Or open Burrow, tap the + button at the top and paste a link.

A new article shows Downloading… under its title for a moment. When that line turns into a reading time, the whole article, pictures included, is stored on your phone.

Read it your way

Open an article and tap the Aa button to change how it looks. You can pick a light, sepia or dark page, choose a font, make the text larger or smaller, and set the line spacing and width until it feels right. Burrow remembers where you stopped, so you can leave in the middle and come back later.

If you would like to listen instead, the read aloud button reads the article out loud while you do something else.

Keep it tidy

Press and hold an article to see what you can do with it. You can mark it as a favorite, move it to the archive when you are done, add tags to find it again, open the original page, or delete it. If you delete something by mistake, the message at the bottom has an Undo button.

  • Favorites keeps the articles you want close.
  • Archive holds what you have finished, out of the way but never lost.
  • Tags group articles by whatever matters to you, and the search finds words inside the articles too.

Bring your reading list with you

If you already keep a reading list somewhere else, open the menu at the top of the library and choose Import. Burrow can read exports from Omnivore and ArchiveBox, and its own export files. Imported links download slowly in the background, a few at a time, and each one is fetched when you open it if it has not been yet.
