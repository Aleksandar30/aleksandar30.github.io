Save from your computer

The Burrow browser extension saves the page you are reading on your computer to Burrow on your Android phone. The page is saved with its images and goes through your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only Burrow can see.

What you need

  • Burrow Pro, with Sync turned on in the app.
  • The same Google account in the extension.

Get the extension

Get it for Firefox

Chrome, Edge and Brave: coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.

How it works

  1. Install the extension.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account you use for Sync.
  3. Click the Burrow button on any page.
