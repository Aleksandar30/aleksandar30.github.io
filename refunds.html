Refunds

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

If Burrow Pro isn't for you, you can get your money back within 30 days of buying it. Each customer can have one refund.

Within 48 hours

Ask Google Play directly. Open your order history in Google Play, find Burrow Pro and choose to request a refund. We don't need to be involved.

After 48 hours, up to 30 days

Write to ivanoviclabs@gmail.com and include your Google Play order number. It starts with GPA, and you can find it in your Google Play order history or in the receipt email from Google. We'll reply and take care of the refund.

Good to know

  • One refund per customer.
  • The 30 days count from the day you bought Pro.
  • Your saved articles stay on your phone, and you can export your library before or after a refund.
  • After a refund, Pro features switch off on your devices; everything free keeps working.
