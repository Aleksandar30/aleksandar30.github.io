The promises

No account

Install it and start saving. There's nothing to sign up for, and no server of ours holds your reading.

Really offline

An article counts as saved only when its text and pictures are on your phone, ready for the bus, a plane or a dead zone.

Free stays free

A free feature never moves behind the paywall. No ads, no tracking, no data sold.

Always exportable

Take your whole library with you at any time, as Markdown, HTML and JSON.
