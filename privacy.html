Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

Burrow doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync. We never receive it.

Who we are

Burrow is made by Aleksandar Ivanović, publishing as Ivanović Labs, in Serbia. You can reach us at ivanoviclabs@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The links you save, the downloaded articles and their images, your tags, highlights, reading positions and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling Burrow deletes them, so export your library first if you want to keep a copy.

Downloading the pages you save

When you save a link, your phone downloads that page directly from the website it belongs to, the same way a browser would. That website sees an ordinary request from your device, including your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Burrow doesn't send these requests through any server of ours.

Sync with Google Drive (Pro)

If you turn on sync, Burrow stores your library data in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Burrow can see. It can't see or touch any of your other Drive files, and only library data goes into the folder. The data travels between your devices and Google; we never receive it. Google's privacy policy applies to your Drive. You can turn sync off at any time, and remove Burrow's Drive data in Google Drive's settings under Manage apps.

The browser extension (Pro)

The Burrow extension for Chrome and Firefox saves the page you are looking at. It passes the captured page to your phone through your own Google Drive, using the same hidden app folder as sync. It doesn't send pages to us or to any other server. Once your phone has first seen a captured page, Burrow removes it from your Drive after 30 days.

Google user data

When you turn on sync or sign in to the extension, Burrow asks Google only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see your other Drive files or your Google account data.

Burrow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the speech voices already installed on your phone. The speech runs on the device: the text of your articles is not sent anywhere, not to us and not to anyone else.

Purchases

Burrow Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. Google tells us only that a purchase was made, so Burrow can unlock Pro. To handle purchases, the app uses Google's Play Billing Library. That library is Google's code and sends Google technical diagnostics about its own operation, such as whether a purchase request succeeded or failed, under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive this information.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use them only to reply. Ask us to delete your messages at any time. If you use Contact support in the app, your mail app opens with a message that already contains the Burrow version and the Android version. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can delete those lines.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address to run and protect the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts.

Children

Burrow isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data.

Your rights

Because we don't hold your data, there's usually nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you think we hold something about you, such as an email you sent, write to us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your data, we'll say so in the app.
